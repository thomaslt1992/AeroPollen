# AeRobiology example data

`munich_pollen.csv` and `POMO_pollen.csv` were converted from the example data
objects distributed with AeRobiology 2.0.4 on CRAN.

The Munich daily observations were provided by the Zentrum Allergie und Umwelt
(ZAUM), including the research team of Prof. Jeroen Buters. The POMO data were
provided by the Bavarian State Office for Health and Food Safety (LGL) and ZAUM
through the ePIN Network.

AeRobiology citation:

> Rojo J, Picornell A, Oteros J (2019). “AeRobiology: the computational tool
> for biological data in the air.” Methods in Ecology and Evolution.
> <https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210X.13203>

The datasets retain the upstream GPL-3 terms in `LICENSE` within this directory.
